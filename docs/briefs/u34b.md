Do — rulings to implement (the lead made these; record any you have to change in your reply):
1. **"On hit"** = a direct hit by the player that lands and deals damage: the basic attack and damaging abilities (each
   target of an AoE counts once per cast). Not DoT ticks, counters, reflects, auras, companions or items. Roll each
   chance on the run Rng in a fixed order (weapon passives in item order).
2. `statusOnHit` → apply the status (duration from the passive) through the normal `applyStatus` (immunities, resist, caps).
3. `curse` (Shadow-Kissed Dagger) → `chance` to Curse (3) the target; **every** on-hit costs the wielder `damage` HP,
   never below 1 (self-costs never kill, D-066 #5). Log it ("The dagger drinks.").
4. `lifeSteal` → heal the player `pct` × the HP damage a rule-1 hit actually dealt (after shields and mitigation), rounded
   down; no overheal. DoT ticks do not steal.
5. `regen` (`hp`/`mp`) → at the start of each of the player's combat turns, before the Wizard's mana regen; HP halved
   while Poisoned like `status_regen`. Out of combat: no effect (travel regen is attunements' job).
6. `custom` scripts: `basic_attack_rider` → a basic attack that lands adds `pct` × its final damage as a separate
   `damageType` hit (cannot miss, no crit, resistance applies, not "on hit"); `damage_vs_status` → +`pct` damage
   multiplier (step "status modifiers") when the target holds `status`, any player direct hit; `reflect_melee` → when a
   non-AoE Physical enemy hit deals HP damage to the player (the engine's existing "melee" proxy, as Riposte uses),
   `pct` × that damage goes back as `damageType` (cannot miss, no crit, not reflected again). Unknown `scriptId`s
   are ignored at combat time (the validator owns them).
7. **Foresight** (`att_foresight`, D-083): while equipped, the player sees next-next intents exactly as `rgr_hunters_eye`
   gives them (reuse the existing mechanism; no new UI).
8. **Horde Rising** (`mod_horde_rising`, D-083): every non-boss encounter gains +1 enemy (max 3) from the region pool; each
   non-boss, non-elite spawn has a 30% chance to be an elite variant (+40% max HP, +20% ATK, ×1.8 XP; name prefixed
   "Elite"). Use the encounter's own Rng stream; bosses and `once` story fights are untouched.
9. Events: emit the existing event types (`damaged`/`healed`/`statusApplied`…) with a `source` naming the item or
   affix (`gear:<itemId>` / `affix:<affixId>`) so U39's damage-share metric can attribute it.
