# Brief U81c: fleeing — you always fall back, and fleeing never loses a quest's items

Agent: **Opus**. Two bugs Tim found playing (D-143). Must land **before U80's batch**. Owns `src/engine/combat/flee.ts`,
the arrival logic that re-fires a held group (`src/engine/reducers/combat.ts`, minimal), encounter `onFlee` data in
`src/content/regions/**`, DESIGN §4.7's flee paragraph (edit it first, then code: the lead has ruled below), tests, pins and
re-recorded traces.

**Bug 1 — fleeing leaves you standing where you were.** Today a successful flee from a location's **fixed** group
(`onFlee: 'scatter'`, the default) scatters the group for the loop and leaves the party *at that node* (DESIGN §4.7). Only
wandering encounters put you back at the previous node. Players expect to fall back the way they came, every time.

**Bug 2 — fleeing can make a quest uncompletable.** `q_void_shards` needs 3 Void Shards: 1 loose in the Hollow's crystal and
2 from Void-Touched Stalkers (100% drop). The Stalkers are the Hollow's fixed group, `enc_hollow_stalkers_a`. Flee them and
the group scatters for the rest of the loop with no loot, so the shards are gone. The journal and Nyx keep sending you east to
an empty Hollow. The same trap can exist wherever an objective needs a drop from a fixed group.

**The lead's ruling:**
- **Every successful flee puts the party at the previous node** (if there is one and it differs; otherwise stay). The flee's
  watches (1, or 3 for a scatter) and the group's fate are unchanged:
  - `scatter` still scatters the group for the loop, so the node is passable next time. Pacifist reachability is kept
    (D-037): the player walks back in and passes.
  - `hold` keeps the group's wounds until dawn and re-fires it on return.
  - `previous` is as today.
- **A fixed group whose creatures drop an item that a quest objective needs never scatters.** Its `onFlee` becomes `hold`, so
  the fight waits, wounded, until dawn and then resets in full. Fleeing costs time, never the quest.
  - Set this in data on every such encounter, starting with `enc_hollow_stalkers_a`. Audit all quests: objectives with an
    `item` condition whose item drops (any chance) from a creature placed in a fixed encounter. List them.
  - Add a **content test** that fails if any such encounter is `scatter`, so future content can't reintroduce the trap.
- Check that a held group re-fires correctly on return (it shows its stored HP), and resets at dawn.

Also check the Hollow's other shard sources still work: the crystal's loose shard, Stalkers in the return-encounter pool, and
Veil drops.
