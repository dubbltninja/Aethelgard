**What Tim reported:**
1. **The movement buttons move between locations.** The exit buttons are a flowing row, so north, south, east and west sit
   in a different place at every location. He wants an **arrow-key style pad in a fixed position** that doesn't move as new
   locations load.
2. **Combat on desktop: Attack and the other buttons jump between moves.** Banners, intent text, ability reasons, toasts and
   callouts change height and push the controls around.
3. **On phones you scroll all over the page** to look at and read things; the layout doesn't feel put together. **The pinned
   bottom bar is perfect, especially in combat: keep it and build on it.**

## Do

**1. The movement pad (all viewports).**
- Replace the exit row with a **fixed compass pad**: a 3×3 grid with **North** top-centre, **West** left, **East** right and
  **South** bottom-centre. The centre is "you are here" or Look around.
- **Up, Down, In, Out, Portal and Path** get their own **fixed** slots around the pad, for example in the corners or a
  second small row; your design, but each always in the same place.
- **Every slot always exists.** A direction with no exit is an empty, disabled slot (named "No way north" for screen
  readers, or hidden from them). Nothing reflows when the set of exits changes.
- Each button shows its destination name, truncated to fit a **fixed button size** (full name on hover/focus and in the
  accessible name), the watch cost and the locked or infected state.
- Arrow keys and WASD map to the pad as today.
- **Where it lives:**
  - **phones** — in the pinned bottom dock during explore, the way the phone action bar works in combat, so movement is
    always under the thumb;
  - **desktop** — a fixed spot (e.g. beside the scene or in a bottom dock) that doesn't depend on how long the location's
    text is.

**2. Combat controls that don't jump (desktop and phone).**
- The turn's controls (Attack, Guard, Item, Flee and the abilities) sit in a **fixed region**: a pinned dock on desktop too,
  or a fixed-height area. Text above them can't push them.
- **Banners** (phase changes, "what changed"), the first-fight callout and toasts appear **as overlays or in reserved
  space**, never inserted above the controls.
- Intent and status text, ability-tile reasons and the Guard preview get **reserved heights**, so a longer line doesn't
  shift its neighbours. Truncate with full text on focus, or use a fixed number of lines.
- At **1280×800** the whole turn (stage, the target's intent, both HP bars, the controls) fits without scrolling (U79 #2).

**3. Phones without page-scrolling.**
- **One screen per view:** a compact HUD, a shorter scene band, and the text in its **own scroll region**. The page itself
  doesn't scroll in explore or combat at 390×844 and 390×664.
- **Explore:** the narration and log scroll inside their panel; NPCs, items and actions are reachable without hunting (for
  example as a tabbed or collapsible strip above the dock).
- Keep the pinned bottom bar (Tim likes it). The explore dock carries the movement pad, plus the action row or a "More"
  sheet.
- **Toasts** don't cover the HUD, the map or narration (U79 #1, D-139). Use one compact stack in a reserved corner that
  collapses to a count, with an auto-dismiss that's shorter on phones.
- Panels (Codex, Inventory, Journal) open as full-height sheets that scroll inside themselves.
- Fix the duplicate `I` in the combat key legend (U79 #9).
- Keep the 1.5× text-size and landscape support Codex added (D-140), and no sideways scroll anywhere.
